# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jarvis for Windows to be a friendly, harassment-free project for
everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behaviour:

- Being kind and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Owning your mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what's best for the people who use and build the project

Not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention
- Insults, trolling, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Publishing someone's private information (an address, phone number, email,
  contacts, or anything from their PC, logs or screenshots) without their
  explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews), and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer,
[@sakethpragallapati](https://github.com/sakethpragallapati), privately
(for example through the email on that GitHub profile). Every report is
reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Depending on how serious it is and whether it repeats, the maintainer may
give a private warning, remove or edit the content, or temporarily or
permanently ban someone from the project.
